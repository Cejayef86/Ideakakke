Delete your IdeaForge account

You can delete your account here without installing the Android app. Use the same Google account you used for IdeaForge.

What will be deleted

  • Your ideas, saved AI results, and comments attached to those ideas.
  • Your comments on other ideas, even if an idea is now private or removed.
  • Your reports, blocked-user settings and terms consent.
  • Your IdeaForge sign-in account in Firebase Authentication.

Your Google/Gmail account is not deleted. Other people's independent copies of shared content and Google's service records are outside this automated cleanup. A minimal account-ID/time marker remains to stop old sessions recreating deleted data. Read the retention details.

Deletion is permanent. Keep this page open and connected until it finishes. If interrupted, sign in with the same account and retry; already deleted data stays deleted. Once cleanup starts, IdeaForge blocks new app data for that account.
