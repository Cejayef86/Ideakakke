Privacy policy

IdeaForge is developed under the OzSmartApps developer name. This policy describes the Android app and its support and deletion website. Contact the IdeaForge operator at IdeaForgeHQ27@gmail.com for privacy questions.

Information we process

  • Google sign-in: Firebase Authentication processes your Google account identity, name, email, profile information made available by Google, and a unique account ID. The app uses your display name to attribute ideas and comments. We do not receive your Google password.
  • App content: titles, descriptions, categories, tags, priorities, public/private settings, collaborator display names, timestamps, comments and saved AI suggestions.
  • Safety and preferences: blocked account IDs, terms consent, report references and the explanation you choose to submit.
  • Security and technical data: Google/Firebase services process information such as IP addresses, app/device integrity signals, authentication tokens and operational logs to deliver and protect the service. Play Integrity and Firebase App Check help reject unauthorised requests.
  • Support: if you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate you, store and synchronise ideas, show public community content, generate suggestions when requested, process reports and deletion, and protect the service. The app has no advertising SDK, paid subscription, or Firebase Analytics integration in this release. We do not sell your personal information.

Private and public content

New ideas are private by default. An idea marked public, its display name, and its comments can be read by other signed-in users. Collaborator names are labels, not access permissions. People may copy content you make public or explicitly share through another app; deletion from IdeaForge cannot erase copies they already made. Do not publish personal information about others.

Optional AI and Google

AI research is optional and off by default when creating an idea. Choosing it sends the idea's title, description, category and tags to Google's Gemini service through Firebase AI Logic. Generated suggestions are saved with the idea. They can be inaccurate and are not verified market research or professional advice.

The current project uses the unpaid Gemini Developer API. Under Google's unpaid-service terms, Google may use prompts and responses to improve products and machine-learning technology, and human reviewers may process them. Do not send confidential, sensitive or personal information to AI. See Google's Gemini API terms and Google's privacy policy. Disabling AI avoids this optional prompt transfer.

Service providers and external links

Google/Firebase provides authentication, database storage, hosting, app verification and AI. These services may process data in countries outside Australia. The main app database is in Sydney; that does not mean every service processes data only in Australia. Marketplace buttons open Facebook Marketplace externally with the selected search text; those sites and apps have their own policies. IdeaForge is not affiliated with Facebook or Meta.

Retention and deletion

Saved content remains until you delete it or your account. Use the account menu's Delete account control, or the web deletion page, to remove your ideas and their discussions, your comments on other ideas (including orphaned comments), reports, blocked-user settings, consent settings and Firebase sign-in account. Cleanup needs an internet connection; if interrupted, sign in with the same Google account and retry. Your Google account itself is not deleted.

A minimal security marker containing the random account ID and deletion-start time remains so old sessions cannot recreate deleted data. It contains no name, email, idea text or report explanation. Google may retain service/security records under its own policies. We cannot promise deletion of independent copies or data already processed under Google's AI terms. Contact support for deletion or access requests that the automated controls cannot resolve.

Device storage and security

Firebase may cache authenticated session and app data on your device. The app excludes its private data from Android backup and transfer. Sign out when using a shared device; remove the app or clear its storage to remove device caches. Network requests use HTTPS and database access rules restrict private data to its owner.

Adults and changes

IdeaForge is intended for people aged 18 or over, and this release is prepared for Australia. It is not directed to children. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal data. We will update this page when the app's practices change.
