What you may share

What is prohibited

Reporting, blocking and moderation

Use the idea's safety menu, the Report AI output control, or a comment's report control to tell us about harmful content. You can block authors to hide their ideas/comments and stop new comments from them on your ideas. Manage blocks in the account menu. You may also email IdeaForgeHQ27@gmail.com.

The operator reviews submitted reports and may remove content or restrict accounts that violate these rules. Reports contain a content reference and your explanation; include useful details. For urgent threats, use appropriate emergency services rather than waiting for app support. Community rules apply equally to AI-generated content shared in the app.
